# s6n22 jobs: exhaustive S6 at |B| = 22

Lane wave6/s6n22, October 5-6, 2026. Rung (b) computation. Nothing leaves this machine.

## What runs

Every simple bipartite 6-regular graph B on 11+11 vertices, generated by nauty genbg in 10,000
res/mod slices, through `code/s6n22` (C). Per graph: exact E10 test (minimum edge cut over all S with
2 <= |S| <= 20; non-E10 graphs are counted and skipped). Per E10 graph, every edge oy (66 tests; Y =
B - o - y is symmetric in o and y, so the 132 ordered pairs give 66 distinct tests): decide whether Y
has two edge-disjoint Hamilton cycles. Every pair claimed is checked directly (two cyclic vertex
sequences over V(B) - o - y, consecutive vertices adjacent in B, the 40 vertex pairs distinct).

Pipeline per test (chosen from the pilot, see below):
- AM sample: the graphs with index % 4 == 0 inside each slice (25% of graphs, all 66 edges) run the
  Asratian-Mirumian frame walk first (random pi, random frame of Y+ = Y + Lambda, legal
  3-transformations and Lambda re-pairings, accept unless Phi rises, else with probability 1/20),
  short budget 64 iterations. A stall saves the frame (`*.stall`), the same walk continues to 1,000
  iterations, then DFS.
- Every other test: DFS first (enumerate Hamilton cycles H1 of Y, complete search for a Hamilton
  cycle of Y - E(H1); 50,000 nodes), then the AM walk (3 fresh walks of 1,000 iterations), then DFS
  with 5,000,000 nodes.
- Anything left goes to `*.surv` and is decided by SAT (`code/sat_check.py`: wave4/va6
  span_oracle.py, MiniSat with lazy cuts; witness re-checked; a non-SPAN answer is re-decided by
  wave5/s6hunt myspan.span2hc, Glucose, and a certificate JSON is written).

Why the DFS goes first (deviation from the brief, which put the recoloring pass first): on the pilot
slice 3333 (2,000 graphs, 132,000 tests) the AM walk costs 30.6 us per test (frame construction about
4 us, about 2 us per move), the DFS 1.4 us per test. The cutoff that minimizes total time per (o, y)
is therefore "no recoloring", so the recoloring pass runs on a fixed 25% sample instead, which is
where the stalled frames for the proof lane come from.

appends a line to LOG.md. To cancel it: `kill 59865`. To run them by hand instead:

1. `code/sat_stalls.sh 8`: SAT on every stalled AM-sample test (about 4 ms each; about 260k lines
   expected), then rewrites RESULT.md with the totals. The first 2,462 stalls were all SPAN
   (`data/val/sat_stalls_early.log`).

   frame is a valid frame with the recorded Phi (the first 4,004 lines: all valid).
